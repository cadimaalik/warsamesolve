About Formula252

Started by Fırat Kara and Ahmet Salih Çetin — two ODTÜ CE students who got tired of spending Friday nights on iteration tables.

We built the tools we wished existed when we started. Every calculator here has been verified against actual ODTÜ lecture notes and textbook examples — not just "looks right" verified, but number-by-number checked.

The name? CE 252. First structures course. The one that made us question our choices.

CODING IS NOT A PREREQUISITE.

You don't need to know JavaScript, React, or anything beyond the engineering itself. Send us pseudocode, Python, a photo of your notebook — we'll handle the rest. Your name goes on the site either way.
