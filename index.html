About Formula252

Formula252 was started by Ali Warsame — 3rd year Civil Engineering student at METU (Middle East Technical University).

Other students are bringing their own expertise to the platform. Fırat Kara brought Numerical Methods. Ahmet Salih Çetin is building Soil Mechanics. The door is open for anyone.

CODING IS NOT A PREREQUISITE.

If you know your subject, you can contribute. You bring the engineering knowledge — the methodology, the formulas, the edge cases. AI handles the code. That's the whole point.

Formula252 is and always will be completely free. No ads, no paywalls, no catches. Just tools that help you pass your courses and actually understand the material.
